# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes contributions and discussion from anyone interested
in incident management, LLM-assisted decision support, or AI governance
design. We're committed to keeping participation harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or
affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, discussions, and pull
  request reviews.
- Assume good faith; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept that this is a small, part-time-maintained portfolio project —
  response times will vary, and that isn't a reflection of how much a
  contribution is valued.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private
  channels connected to this project.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions)
and to representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a
confidential issue or contacting the maintainer directly via the contact
method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and addressed as
promptly as reasonably possible. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
contributions or comments that violate this Code of Conduct, and may
temporarily or permanently restrict access for repeated or serious
violations.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is a lightweight adaptation of common open-source
community standards (e.g. the Contributor Covenant), simplified for the
scale of this project.
